Governance

How Siraj Center is structured and held accountable.

Structure

Organizational Structure

Siraj Center is governed by its own Board of Directors, who are responsible for the organization's strategic direction and fiduciary oversight. Officers are elected by the Board to carry out day-to-day operations.

Governing Body
Board of Directors

Key Principles

  • Siraj Center is a legally independent nonprofit organization.
  • The Board of Directors provides governance and strategic oversight.
  • Officers are elected by the Board to manage operations.
  • All decisions are guided by a commitment to fiduciary responsibility.
  • Board members serve on a volunteer basis, without compensation.

Strategic Direction

The Board sets long-term priorities and ensures the organization remains mission-focused.

Fiduciary Oversight

Ensuring responsible, transparent management of the organization's finances and resources.

Accountability

As a 501(c)(3), Siraj Center is publicly accountable and committed to ethical governance.
