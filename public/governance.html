Governance & Oversight

How Siraj Center is structured, accountable, and aligned with its mission.

Structure

Organizational Structure

Siraj Center is a legally separate nonprofit organization that operates with its own Board of Directors. The Mountain House Muslim Association (MHMA) provides governance oversight, appoints the Board, and ensures fiduciary responsibility and mission alignment.

Key Governance Principles

  • Siraj Center is a legally separate nonprofit organization from MHMA.
  • The MHMA Board of Trustees provides governance and strategic oversight.
  • MHMA Trustees have the authority to appoint and remove the Siraj Center Board of Directors.
  • Siraj Center officers are elected by its own Board of Directors.
  • All operations are guided by a commitment to fiduciary responsibility and mission alignment.
  • Board members serve on a volunteer basis, without compensation.
