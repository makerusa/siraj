About Siraj Center

Who we are, our legal standing, and our place in the community.

Our Identity

A Community Organization with Purpose

Siraj Center is a separate California nonprofit religious corporation recognized as a 501(c)(3) organization by the Internal Revenue Service. We were established to support religious, educational, and community-centered initiatives that serve the Muslim community in California and beyond.

The name Siraj (سراج) means "lamp" or "light" in Arabic — a symbol of guidance, knowledge, and illumination. We strive to be a light for our community, providing resources, programming, and support that uplift and connect.
