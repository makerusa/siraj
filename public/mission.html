Mission & Vision

The values and aspirations that guide Siraj Center.

Our Purpose

Why We Exist

🌟 Our Mission

To support religious, educational, and community-centered initiatives that serve the Muslim community in Mountain House and beyond.

🔭 Our Vision

A thriving, well-connected Muslim community — grounded in faith, engaged in civic life, and supported by institutions that serve with integrity and compassion.

Our Core Values

🕌

Faith

Rooted in Islamic principles of justice, compassion, and service.

📚

Knowledge

Promoting learning as a core religious and communal obligation.

⚖️

Integrity

Upholding honesty and ethical standards in every decision.

🌍

Community

Building bonds of brotherhood, sisterhood, and civic engagement.

💡

Enlightenment

Being a lamp of guidance — the meaning of Siraj.
