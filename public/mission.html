Mission & Vision

The values and aspirations that guide Siraj Center.

Our Purpose

Why We Exist

🌟 Our Mission

Siraj Center exists to support religious, educational, and community-centered initiatives that serve the Muslim community in California and beyond. We are committed to responsible stewardship of resources, mission alignment with MHMA, and meaningful impact in the lives of community members.

🔭 Our Vision

We envision a thriving, well-connected Muslim community in Mountain House and the surrounding region — one grounded in faith, engaged in civic life, and supported by institutions that serve with integrity and compassion.

Focus Areas

Religious Services

Facilitating worship, Islamic study circles, Friday programs, and spiritual development opportunities for the community.

Youth Education

Providing structured Islamic education and enrichment for the next generation of Muslim Americans.

Community Support

Organizing events, resources, and programs that address the real needs of Muslim families in our area.
